# rex

[rex](https://rex.debridmediamanager.com) is a Plex Media Server that owns no files. The official Plex apps see it as a normal Plex server. Another program tells rex what is in your library and serves the files, and when you press play rex streams the file straight from that program.

- [rex with zurg](with-zurg.md) sets rex up from scratch, with zurg filling it from your debrid account
- [rex with your own app](any-source.md) sets up the same server filled by any app that serves files over HTTP and speaks rex's feed API
- [Building rex addons](addons.md) brings home videos, recordings or Stremio catalogs into rex with a program of your own
- [Bring your watch history](import-watched.md) copies what you watched, where you stopped and your ratings from your old Plex server
- [Kometa with rex](kometa.md) builds collections and poster badges on your rex libraries
- [Apps download from your provider](direct-download.md) has Plex apps download from your debrid service themselves, so the rex machine carries no movie

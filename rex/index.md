# rex

[rex](https://rex.debridmediamanager.com) is a media server for the official Plex apps that owns no files. The apps see it as a normal Plex server. Another program tells rex what is in your library and serves the files, and when you press play rex streams the file straight from that program. rex is free for personal use, and its [source code](https://github.com/debridmediamanager/rex) is public under the PolyForm Noncommercial license.

- [rex questions](faq.md) answers what to know before you install
- [rex with zurg](with-zurg.md) sets rex up from scratch, with zurg filling it from your debrid account
- [rex with your own app](any-source.md) sets up the same server filled by any app that serves files over HTTP and speaks rex's feed API
- [rex with your Stremio addons](stremio.md) fills rex from your Stremio addons' catalogs with rex-stremio, with no zurg and no sponsorship
- [Move from Plex to rex](from-plex.md) runs rex beside or instead of your Plex server, and says what to keep and what to turn off in zurg
- [Share rex with family and friends](sharing.md) invites the people you watch with
- [Building rex addons](addons.md) brings home videos, recordings or Stremio catalogs into rex with a program of your own
- [Bring your watch history](import-watched.md) copies what you watched, where you stopped and your ratings from your old Plex server
- [Kometa with rex](kometa.md) builds collections and poster badges on your rex libraries
- [Apps download from your provider](direct-download.md) has Plex apps download from your debrid service themselves, so the rex machine carries no movie

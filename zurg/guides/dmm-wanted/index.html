Fetch what you want from DMM

Press Want on a film or show on Debrid Media Manager, and your zurg fetches it. There is no Sonarr or Radarr to set up and nothing to mount. Shows keep getting their new episodes as they air.

It is for sponsors: zurg reads your list with your DMM API key.

What you need

  • A zurg nightly from 2026-10-25 or later.

  • Your DMM API key in zurg's config.yml. It is the key you get for sponsoring DMM:

    dmm_api_key: YOUR_DMM_API_KEY
  • Something for zurg to fetch onto: a debrid account that takes torrents, an nzb provider for Usenet, or both.

That is the whole setup. zurg reads your list once a minute from the moment it starts.

Adding titles

  1. Paste the same key on DMM's Settings page.

  2. Open a film and press Want. On a show's season page, Want S2 adds that season and Want all seasons adds the whole show, new seasons included.

  3. Your list is at debridmediamanager.com/wanted. It says when your zurg last read it, and when it fetched each film.

Requests from Seerr, Overseerr or Jellyseerr can land on the same list: see Seerr through DMM.

Following a list

Paste the page of a Trakt or MDBList list on your wanted list and press Follow. Every film and show on it is wanted, and DMM reads the list again every six hours, so what is added to it later is wanted too. Pages it takes:

  • https://trakt.tv/users/<user>/lists/<list> and https://trakt.tv/lists/<id>
  • https://trakt.tv/users/<user>/watchlist, if that Trakt profile is public
  • https://mdblist.com/lists/<user>/<list>

On DMM's own Trakt pages, your watchlist and each of your lists have a Follow with Want button that does the same.

An IMDb, Letterboxd, Rotten Tomatoes or Plex watchlist: on mdblist.com, make an external list from its URL and follow that. MDBList keeps it in step with the source.

What following does:

  • A show is wanted whole, new seasons included.

  • Only titles new on the list since it was last read are added. A title you remove from your wanted list stays removed, and a title that leaves the list stays wanted. Unfollow keeps what the list added.

  • The page shows each list with how many titles it has, how many it added so far and when it was last read. Read again reads it now. Titles without an IMDb id are left out, and the count says how many.

  • Your list holds 5,000 titles. A list that keeps growing, like a trending one, fills it.

Moving from Radarr or Sonarr

Bring over everything they are still looking for:

  1. In Radarr or Sonarr, open System → Backup, press Backup Now and download the zip. The scheduled backups in its Backups folder work too.

  2. On your wanted list, press Import a Radarr or Sonarr backup and pick the zip. Then do the same with the other app's backup.

What comes over:

  • From Radarr, every monitored film without a file that has reached its minimum availability. Films with no IMDb id are left out; the page says how many.

  • From Sonarr, every monitored show with aired episodes missing, with just those seasons.

  • Titles already on your list stay as they are.

Only the database inside the zip is uploaded. The rest of the backup, the app's API key included, stays on your computer.

Two things do not come over:

  • Quality profiles. zurg chooses releases by its own acquisition settings. Radarr usually refuses camera and telesync copies; to keep them out, add them to exclude_words:

    acquisition:
  exclude_words: [CAM, CAMRip, HDCAM, TS, HDTS, TELESYNC, TC, HDTC, TELECINE]

  • Sonarr's season numbers, where they differ. Your list numbers seasons as TMDB does. Sonarr follows TheTVDB, which splits some long-running anime into more seasons, so a Sonarr season that TMDB does not have is not fetched. Check those shows on their DMM page and press Want on the seasons you are missing.

What zurg fetches

  • A film once. It then leaves the list zurg reads. With upgrades: true under acquisition: in zurg's config, a film fetched below the resolution quality heads for (2160p for best) stays on the list, and zurg fetches a better copy when one comes out. The copy you already have is kept. /wanted shows what zurg took and the resolution it is still waiting for.

  • A show: every aired episode of the seasons you picked, as a season pack where one covers the season and as single episodes where it is still airing. It keeps checking for new episodes until you remove the show. Specials are not fetched.

Releases come from Usenet as NZBs and from torrents your debrid account already has cached; uncached torrents are never added. If you have not set up any indexers, zurg searches DMM's own. Quality, size limits, words to avoid and the audio and subtitle languages a release must have are zurg's acquisition settings, the same for every source.

Watching it in Kodi or Plex

With __magic__ on, zurg files what it fetches by title, into __magic__/__movies__ and __magic__/__shows__, named the way Kodi and Plex match it. Point a Movies library and a TV library at those two folders: see __movies__ and __shows__.

magic:
  enabled: true

Two zurgs on one key

Each zurg reads the same list, so two of them fetch every title twice. Keep the list on one and turn it off on the others:

acquisition:
  sources:
    - name: dmm-wanted
      type: dmm
      enabled: false

When it stops

If your sponsorship lapses or you reset your key, zurg logs that DMM refused the key and asks again every hour. What it already fetched stays in your library.

More detail: DMM wanted list in the acquisition reference.

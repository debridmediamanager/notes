What each piece does

One line for every piece of DMM, zurg and rex, from DMM Cast and the Kodi addon to rex-stremio, the Jellyfin and Emby plugins and the words you meet in the guides.

The names don't say what anything does, so here is one line for each. Anything not marked For sponsors is free. To find which ones you need, use Choose your setup.

The three projects

Name What it does
DMM (Debrid Media Manager) A website for finding releases and adding them to your debrid account. It also searches Usenet and Easynews
zurg Every debrid account and Usenet as one library. Plex, Jellyfin and Emby read it as a folder, Infuse and Kodi over WebDAV, and rex gets it directly. Current builds are for sponsors
rex A media server for the Plex apps with no files of its own. zurg or an addon tells it what there is. Free for personal use

Watching straight from DMM

No server needed for any of these.

Name What it does
DMM Cast A Stremio addon that plays releases from your debrid or Easynews account. It works in Nuvio too
DMM Kodi addon Browse, search and play from your debrid or Easynews account inside Kodi
DMM Watch A button on DMM that opens a file in your own player, like VLC or Infuse

Feeding rex

Name What it does
rex-stremio Turns your Stremio addons into rex libraries, so they play in Plex apps
rex addons Small programs that give rex something to play: YouTube, Spotify, IPTV, home videos, or whatever you write
rex-live-sports A rex addon that puts live sports channels and a guide in the Plex apps' Live TV
rex-direct Gives your rex its own secure address, so Plex apps reach it away from home
rex for Android rex and its addons on an Android phone or TV box, no computer
zurg+rex for Android A Plex server on your phone: zurg and rex together on one Android device, with your debrid and Usenet library. For sponsors

Inside Jellyfin and Emby

Name What it does
Debrid plugins (RD zurg, AD zurg, TB zurg, PM zurg, OC zurg, DL zurg) One plugin per debrid provider: Real-Debrid, AllDebrid, TorBox, Premiumize, Offcloud and Debrid-Link. Your debrid library shows up in Jellyfin or Emby with no zurg app or mount. For sponsors
NZB zurg A Jellyfin plugin that streams NZBs through your own Usenet account. For sponsors

Adding titles for you

Name What it does
Wanted Press Want on DMM and your zurg fetches it, new episodes included. For sponsors
Seerr through DMM Family requests in Seerr go to your Wanted list. For sponsors
Torznab and Newznab DMM as the indexer for Sonarr, Radarr and Prowlarr. For sponsors
zurg as the download client zurg answers Sonarr and Radarr as qBittorrent for torrents and SABnzbd for Usenet, so you run neither. For sponsors
zurg's Stremio addon Searches Usenet when you press play in Stremio and streams it through zurg. For sponsors
__magic__ A zurg folder you arrange yourself, or let Sonarr and Radarr arrange. For sponsors

The rest of DMM

Name What it does
Usenet tab Sends a Usenet release to your Real-Debrid library, with no Usenet account. Sponsors go first and get a bigger daily allowance
Request board Ask for a release Real-Debrid doesn't have. Another DMM user moves it into your library. Fill 10 requests and get 7 days of the DMM sponsor perks
Hash lists Share a list of releases so others can add them to their own accounts
Browser extension Adds DMM buttons to movie and TV sites and next to magnet links. For Chrome, Firefox and Safari
gatekeeper The sponsor site. Sponsor there, link your Patreon or GitHub Sponsors pledge and get your DMM API key. What a sponsorship gives you

Words you'll see in the guides

Word What it means
Release One copy of a film, episode or season as someone published it. A title usually has many, in different qualities
Debrid provider A paid service that downloads torrents for you and lets you stream them: Real-Debrid, AllDebrid, TorBox, Premiumize, Offcloud, Debrid-Link
Cached The provider already has the release, so it plays right away
Usenet Another place to get releases. Use your own Usenet account, let TorBox Pro or Easynews do it, or have DMM send a result to Real-Debrid
Easynews A paid Usenet service with its own search that plays files straight over the web
NZB A small file that says where a Usenet release is, like a torrent file does for torrents
Indexer A search site for releases. Sonarr and Radarr use one to find things
Sonarr, Radarr and Prowlarr Apps that fetch titles for you: Sonarr for shows, Radarr for films. Prowlarr keeps their indexers in one place
Seerr A request site for family and friends. Overseerr and Jellyseerr work the same way
Stremio, Nuvio and Kodi Media player apps. Addons give them things to play. Nuvio takes Stremio addons
DMM API key A sponsor's key from gatekeeper. Paste it where a guide asks, and keep it private like a password
Nightly The newest build of zurg or rex, made every night. zurg's nightlies are for sponsors
WebDAV A way to share files over the web. Infuse, Kodi and many TV players can open it
Mount Makes zurg's folder look like a local drive, so Plex, Jellyfin or Emby can scan it
rclone The tool zurg uses to make the mount. zurg's setup gets it for you
.strm A small file that holds a link in place of the video, for players like Kodi
Source Anything that tells rex what it has and serves the files: zurg, rex-stremio or an addon
Claim Putting rex on a Plex account, so it can show titles and posters and Plex apps can find it
Plex Media Server vs Plex apps The server holds your library. The apps on your TV and phone play it. rex replaces the server, not the apps

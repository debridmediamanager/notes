Which setup should I use?

Find your setup with the interactive questionnaire. Answer a few questions about your player accounts and hosting. It returns routes requirements and setup links. No login needed.

Start with the app you want to watch in. You do not need every piece. DMM helps you choose releases. A provider supplies the files. zurg can turn them into a shared library. rex presents a library to Plex apps. Some routes skip a piece.

This map covers supported routes checked on 10 November 2026. It includes outside alternatives that remove a step. Third-party addons and devices can still differ. Follow the linked guide and its tested limits.

Choose where the files come from

An indexer finds releases. A torrent or NZB describes how to fetch one. A provider supplies the bytes. A player decodes them. Access to one does not include the others.

zurg supports Real-Debrid, AllDebrid, TorBox, Premiumize, Offcloud and Debrid-Link. You can combine accounts in one library. Caches quotas and file availability still differ. A cached badge is evidence, not a playback guarantee. See providers.

Choose TorBox hosted NZBs when you want TorBox to download and repair. DMM sends the NZB there. Current zurg nightlies use provider type torbox_nzb and accept NZBs from the watch directory acquisition and SABnzbd interface. TorBox hosted Usenet has its zurg setup. A cache miss must finish before it plays. Downloads share slots with torrents. Retained files can expire, and zurg does not promise to fetch an expired release again. Keep the NZB for another fetch.

Choose direct Usenet when zurg should read through your own news accounts including fallback or block accounts. It uses nzb with NNTP credentials. A TorBox news-server login belongs here too. It is separate from TorBox hosted NZBs. See TorBox NNTP and its plans.

One zurg instance uses either nzb or torbox_nzb. Both own the NZB watch directory. Run both with separate instances and working directories. With dmm_api_key on both, turn the dmm-wanted source off on one, or every Wanted title is fetched twice. Either can sit beside torrent providers. zurg's Stremio addon posts its pick to whichever NZB account the instance has. With torbox_nzb only a release TorBox already holds starts within the addon's wait, and only when no other NZB post is waiting.

Choose DMM → Real-Debrid when you want the release in your RD library. It is a hosted transfer with queues and allowances. An uncached job takes time. It does not add native NNTP support to Real-Debrid or to other debrid providers. The DMM result and Transfers page show progress.

Easynews Watch and Cast play its HTTP files, and Download NZB saves the release's NZB. Send to zurg puts that NZB on your sponsor Wanted list. That needs a configured NZB backend. An Easynews login in DMM does not configure zurg's NZB account.

What about HTTP links?

DMM's TorBox Add dialog accepts direct HTTP(S) links as web downloads. zurg exposes them in __downloads__. An item there is not automatically an identified rex library item. Real-Debrid unrestricted downloads can appear there too. DMM TorBox Cast includes completed web and Usenet downloads and torrents. A web page may still need another integration because of logins expiring URLs DRM or its own player.

rex supports streamable HTTP(S) media through its feed API. A source or addon sends the media address and metadata. rex then makes it available in Plex apps. The file server and feed producer can be separate. No zurg or debrid account is required. rex-stremio does this for compatible Stremio addons. Raw magnets and torrent-only streams do not work. HLS needs rex's FFmpeg setup. rex uses the identity its source supplies.

Acquisition and playback are separate choices. You can change how a title is requested without replacing the app you watch in.

For the recommended DMM + zurg arr route DMM is the indexer and zurg is the download client. DMM offers Torznab and Newznab. Both need active sponsorship. Prowlarr is optional. zurg provides qBittorrent and SABnzbd interfaces. You do not need those downloader apps beside zurg. zurg can also search for arr.

If you want complete local copies download the files from your provider or zurg. You can also use a player's offline-download feature or a normal downloader and disk library. Check quotas and play the saved files without the network. zurg virtual files .strm links and local debrid catalogs are not offline copies. Local catalogs store records only. Playback still depends on the provider.

The tradeoffs that matter before installing

Question What changes your choice
Must another machine stay on? Hosted Cast/Kodi routes, provider WebDAV and DMM Watch do not need your own server. zurg, rex, rex-stremio and media-server plugins do need their host while you watch. zurg+rex Android can make an Android device the host for zurg and rex; rex-stremio and media-server plugins still need a normal host. A rented/managed host changes who runs it, not the underlying route.
Will my TV play the file? WebDAV and addon routes depend on the player's codecs, audio and subtitles. zurg serves bytes; it is not a video transcoder. Plex Media Server, Jellyfin, Emby or rex with FFmpeg can convert when needed, with appropriate hardware and server/app capabilities.
How much disk space? Streaming avoids storing the whole collection. Allow space for databases, artwork, caches and any repair, transcode or offline-download work. “No full download first” does not mean zero disk use.
Who pays for bandwidth? Provider traffic quotas still apply. A server proxy carries playback traffic and uses its outgoing bandwidth; direct playback shifts the connection to the viewer. Library analysis can read remote files too.
Will it work away from home? Hosted addons avoid exposing your own server. Self-hosted services need a securely reachable address; a LAN address alone will not work remotely. See rex remote access and the Stremio HTTPS guidance.
Can family use it simultaneously? Check your provider's IP/concurrency rules and the server's sharing model. Real-Debrid use must stay on one public IP; direct links to different households can violate that even when a central library supplied them. Do not enable redirects without understanding where the downloads originate.
What do I pay for? Keep provider subscriptions, indexer access, DMM sponsorship, hosting and player/server purchases separate. DMM's basic website and rex for personal use are free; rex's Android build comes only inside the sponsor-only zurg+rex app. Current zurg nightlies and DMM's plugin distribution are sponsor benefits; Wanted and DMM's indexer APIs also need sponsorship. rex-stremio does not require it. What a sponsorship gives you lists every perk and where to set it up.
Does a catalog entry guarantee playback? No. Addon catalogs describe titles; cloud files can expire; torrents can lack sources; Usenet articles can be missing. Pick the route whose failure and waiting behavior you are comfortable with.

A compatible server client can also be a third-party app. Infuse connects to Plex Emby and Jellyfin. You may not need a separate zurg mount. Check the plugin and client together.

Infuse supports WebDAV and other network shares. A .strm file gives a compatible player such as Kodi a URL. It is not a universal mount replacement. Check the client and expect different proxy and failover behavior. Do not use .strm as the normal Plex Media Server setup.

There is also a legacy free zurg release. It is Real-Debrid only with a different config format. The multi-provider Usenet and Android routes here use sponsor nightlies.

rex has its own compatibility boundary. It is an independent Plex app server. It is not Plex Media Server or a Plex plugin. Use its FAQ for tested devices transcoding remote use and the fresh-account recommendation. A traditional Plex server can run beside rex.

Other media and existing setups

  • Music: DMM Music provides Real-Debrid browser playback/downloads. rex music sources feed Plexamp and supported Plex apps. A source can be an addon or zurg configured for music. Do not assume a movie/TV addon supplies music too.
  • Live TV: rex's Live TV route provides channels and a guide in the documented apps. Live channels from rex-stremio instead form a library of channels; that is not the same guide interface. Check source availability. rex does not provide DVR recording.
  • Home videos, recordings and photos: use an existing media server, or a rex source you control. The home-video tutorial shows the latter. zurg can also supply comic books as photos to rex; app support differs.
  • Existing Kodi/Stremio addons: if yours already gives the experience you want, keep it. DMM can be the place you select and manage releases without replacing your player. Addon support for providers, Usenet and HTTP varies; the DMM route is not a compatibility promise for all addons.
  • Another debrid mount/downloader already installed: see migration choices before switching. A working setup does not need another layer solely because it exists.
  • Native apps in development: Usenet TV is not a released app-store setup to recommend here. Yomu TV is a separate reading/audiobook project, not a route for this movie/TV stack. Neither is required for the routes above.

Five common starting points

  1. “I have Stremio and RD.” Start with DMM Cast. Add zurg only when you want a shared library, automation or another consumer that needs it.
  2. “I have TorBox Pro and want the NZBs in that post.” Send the NZB to TorBox through DMM. Use TorBox Cast to watch in Stremio, the Kodi addon to watch in Kodi, or zurg's hosted NZB backend to feed your chosen library/player route.
  3. “I already have Jellyfin.” Start with the plugin for your provider. Choose separate zurg when you need its shared filesystem, automation or broader backend capabilities.
  4. “I want Plex apps, no mounts, and my existing addons.” Use rex-stremio with rex. Use zurg as a source instead when the thing you want to expose is your managed debrid/Usenet library. Both sources can feed the same rex.
  5. “I have news accounts and want Sonarr/Radarr.” Use DMM Newznab or your existing indexers, zurg's NZB/SABnzbd route and a playback route from the first table. For permanent local files, choose a normal downloader or explicitly save complete copies; virtual imports alone are not offline files.

Still unsure? Tell us your device or player, existing accounts, whether a host can stay on, and whether you want on-demand playback or an automatic library. Those answers usually decide the route. Getting help says where to ask.
